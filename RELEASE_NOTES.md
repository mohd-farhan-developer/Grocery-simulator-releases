# Grocery Simulator 4.2

Customers are completely rebuilt: faster, busier shopping, checkout desks you can buy, and clearer TalkBack updates.

## Customers

- Customers have been rebuilt from scratch. Each one arrives with their own plans, which you discover only by watching what they do.
- They only want products you have already bought for the store. Something you bought but have not put on a rack counts as missing.
- Customers walk up to the entrance and take a basket there. If none is free, they wait outside and leave after a while.
- Inside, they visit only the racks they need, each in their own order. Quick shoppers take 3 to 5 game minutes, most take 5 to 8, and families take 8 to 10.
- Items leave the rack and show as Reserved the moment a customer picks them.
- If one or two things are sold out, customers carry on. If three are sold out, they put everything back, return the basket, and leave without buying.
- After paying or giving up, customers return their basket and walk out.

## A busier store

- The store now fills toward a steady number of shoppers: 2 to 4 when it is quiet, 4 to 6 at medium times, and 6 to 8 at peak hours.
- New customers arrive a few minutes apart, about 10 to 12 an hour, never all at once.

## Checkout desks

- A new game starts with one checkout desk. Buy a Checkout Desk in MarketHub or BuildMax and assemble it from its box to add Desk 2, then Desk 3. Saved games also start again from one desk.
- Each desk has its own line of up to three customers. Customers choose an empty desk with a cashier first, otherwise the shortest line, otherwise they wait.
- Desks with a working cashier take customers. When no cashier is working, Desk 1 is open for you.
- Moving a cashier to a desk opens it straight away, and the first waiting customer walks over. If a desk closes, the customers waiting in its line move to an open desk.
- BuildMax shows how many checkout desks you have.

## TalkBack

- You will hear when a customer is waiting at checkout, is being served, is getting impatient, or leaves without purchasing.
- When a cashier finishes a sale you will hear, for example: Payment completed for Rahul.
- Queue positions, shopping lists, and walking are never announced.

## Behind the scenes

- Every customer now has a hidden satisfaction level. It is saved with your game and will later shape your store rating, experience, and reviews.
