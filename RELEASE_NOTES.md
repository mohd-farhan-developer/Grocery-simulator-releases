# Grocery Simulator 3.3

Hire staff with HireNow, a new game clock, packet storing, a smarter StockPro, and a cleaner look with toolbar navigation.

## HireNow: hire your staff

- Open HireNow on the Computer to hire cashiers, stock clerks, and cleaners. Each employee has an employee ID, a daily salary, a level, and a status.
- Duty Manager assigns shifts of up to 12 hours with sliders for start hour and length. Staff without a shift stay in Staff Housing.
- Staff walk in, work, take a 15-minute break, another 15-minute break, and a 30-minute lunch at random times, and walk back to Staff Housing. You hear when a shift starts, when breaks start and end, and when a shift finishes.
- Stock clerks carry one box at a time from the warehouse and stock racks one packet at a time. Cleaners patrol and clean. Cashiers wait at the checkout until customers arrive.
- Employees earn XP while working and reach Level 2, Experienced, and Level 3, Expert. Higher levels work 10 and 20 percent faster. You hear when someone levels up.
- Staff Housing shows what each employee is doing, how much shift time is left, and their level.
- Employee Records shows each employee's ID, hire day, days worked, level, total XP, and total salary paid.
- Salaries are paid automatically at the end of each day as BillDesk bills, with an EasyBank transaction and receipt. If cash is short, the salary bill stays due.
- Firing an employee ends their duty at once. Their salary for that day is still paid, and their record is removed.

## Game clock

- The game now has a clock. One real second is one game minute, and it only runs while you are playing.
- Each day runs from 06:00 to midnight and then ends automatically. End Day still skips to the next morning.
- The Shop Floor shows the current time.

## Carrying packets

- While carrying loose packets, choose an opened box of the same product to Store 1 Packet or Store All Packets. A box never goes over its size.
- A box of a different product says which product it is for, and a full box says it is full.

## StockPro

- StockPro now lists only products you have bought. With nothing bought, it tells you to shop in MarketHub first.
- Each product reads its name, total stock, and status first.
- Order from MarketHub on a product's page opens that product in MarketHub, ready to choose a quantity and add to the cart.

## New look and navigation

- Every screen now has a toolbar with a Back button and the page title. The old Back buttons at the bottom are gone.
- The toolbar Back button and your phone's Back gesture do the same thing. On the Shop Floor, Back asks whether to return to the main menu.
- Back no longer takes you through the same screen twice, and returning to the main menu no longer lets Back reopen the game.
- The Computer apps and MarketHub products are shown as cards in two columns, with more space between cards.
- Computer apps, products, employees, and furniture have small pictures. The pictures are hidden from TalkBack, so announcements stay the same.
