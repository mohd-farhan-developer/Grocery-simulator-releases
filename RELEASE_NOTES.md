# Grocery Simulator 4.8

Shoplifters at the door, a customer service counter, spills on the floor, and a day that opens and closes properly.

## Shoplifters

- A few customers now mean to walk out without paying, and nothing gives them away. They shop like anybody else, then head straight past the tills.
- The gate goes off once as they reach the door, and Exit Gate appears on the Shop Floor.
- Check Receipt puts every unpaid packet back on the shelf it came from and sends them on their way. Somebody carrying nothing is simply cleared.
- Let Go waves them through, and that stock never comes back.
- Only one at a time, and the checkouts, the warehouse and the computer all keep working while it waits.

## Customer service

- There is a customer service counter by the entrance now. It tells you whether anybody is waiting.
- About one shopper in twenty gives up looking and comes to it. You are told once, and it never interrupts what you are doing.
- Take Me There walks them to the rack yourself and pleases them most. Give Directions sends them on their own. Send Away costs you.
- A price check reads the shelf against the system. If the two disagree, the shelf is put right and the shop is better thought of for it.

## Spills

- Every so often milk, cooking oil, juice or flour goes over in front of a rack.
- That one rack reads Spill Detected. You find it at the aisle, and it is announced once when you get there.
- A wet floor sign goes out at once and stops customers picking their way around it. The mop takes three seconds and takes the sign with it.
- Leaving it costs the shop a little every minute. Clearing it within two minutes is noticed.

## Opening and closing

- Open Store cannot be pressed twice. The status reads Opening Store while the sound plays, and the first customer is a minute or two behind the doors.
- The closing sound now waits for the last customer to leave and plays just before the report, instead of the moment you press Close Store.
- The End of Day report adds the times you opened and closed, the longest the checkout line got, and how happy the day's customers were on average.

## Computer

- MarketHub and StockPro open with the cursor already in the search box, so one swipe reaches the first product.
- The BillDesk summary is now a button. Choosing it opens the first bill still due.
- StockPro reads in short sentences: total stock, warehouse stock, rack stock, then status.
- HireNow's cards count what is actually happening: hired, working, idle, records.
