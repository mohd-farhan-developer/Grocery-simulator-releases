# Grocery Simulator 4.7

Shelves that remember what they sell, a front side that comes to you, and stock clerks who split the work.

## Shelves keep their labels

- A rack that sells out stays what it was. It is still a Rice rack at its own price, reading Out of Stock and waiting to be filled again.
- You no longer have to place a product on a rack a second time just because it ran empty. Carry stock to it, or let a stock clerk do it.
- Remove Product is the only thing that takes a label off. It asks first, and it only works once the shelf is clear. After that the rack will take any product again.

## The front of a shelf

- Go to Front Side no longer opens a page. It opens the shelf underneath the rack it belongs to, so you keep your place in the aisle.
- Change Price has lost its slider. It opens a box with the current price already filled in and selected, so typing replaces it. Confirm announces the new price and puts you back on Change Price.

## Less to listen to

- Freshness is only read out when the stock has a fortnight or less left. Above that it is not mentioned at all.
- Reserved is only read out when something is actually reserved.

## Stock clerks

- Three stock clerks used to choose the same rack and walk to it together. Each rack is now claimed by one clerk the moment they set off, so three clerks fill three different shelves.
- The emptiest shelf, measured against how big it is, gets seen to first.
- When only one shelf needs filling, one clerk fills it and the others get on with something else instead of following.
