# Grocery Simulator 3.0

The biggest update yet: racks, stocking, storage upgrades, a new Computer desktop, and BillDesk bills.

## Racks and stocking

- Buy Small, Medium, and Large Grocery Racks in MarketHub under Furniture. They hold 20, 40, and 60 items.
- Racks arrive as rack boxes in the Warehouse. Choose Assemble in Shop, then Assemble Rack on the Shop Floor.
- Every rack has a Front Side for customers and a Back Side for stocking.
- On the Back Side, open a carried box and press Place 1 Packet. Each press moves exactly one packet.
- The first product placed on an empty rack names the rack and copies the product's selling price onto its price tag.
- A rack only accepts its own product. Bringing the wrong product says which product the rack is assigned to.
- Racks show a status: In Stock, Low Stock, Full, or Empty. A low stock warning is spoken once when stock drops to 5.
- When a rack runs out, it turns back into an Empty Rack automatically.
- Change a rack's price from its Front Side with a slider.

## Warehouse and boxes

- Select any box to open its own page with only the actions that make sense for it.
- Sealed boxes can be opened or carried. Opened boxes let you carry them or take up to 10 packets with a slider.
- The Warehouse and Shop Floor show what you are carrying. You can carry one thing at a time.
- Put Box Down leaves a box on the Shop Floor, keeping its packets. Floor boxes can be returned to the Warehouse.
- Empty cardboard boxes stay until you throw them away.

## Storage capacity and upgrades

- The Warehouse holds 30 boxes and the Shop Floor holds 8 boxes to start. Racks do not count.
- A delivery that does not fit is not received until you free up space or upgrade.
- Buy Warehouse Expansion for 30 more boxes, or Shop Floor Expansion for 4 more floor boxes.

## New Computer desktop

- The Computer is now a desktop with six apps: MarketHub, EasyBank, BillDesk, StockPro, HireNow, and BuildMax.
- MarketHub is the Market. StockPro is the Inventory. BuildMax shows your storage space and upgrades.
- EasyBank and HireNow are coming in a later update.

## BillDesk bills

- Pay your supermarket's bills from BillDesk on the Computer.
- Shop Rent 1,200 rupees, Electricity Bill 380 rupees, and Water Bill 300 rupees are due every 7 days. Internet Bill 999 rupees is due every 30 days.
- Each bill reads its amount, due day, and status in one announcement.
- Bills paid late get a late fee: 10 percent for rent, 8 percent for electricity, and 5 percent for water and internet. The fee is announced once.
- Turn on Auto Pay in a bill's page to pay it automatically on its due day when you have enough cash.
- Every payment saves a receipt with a transaction number.
- The first time you enter the Shop Floor each day, you hear how many bills are due today or overdue.

## Days

- Press End Day on the Shop Floor to start the next day. You hear the new day, overdue bills, and new bills in one announcement.
- End Day is temporary until opening and closing the store is added.

## Accessibility

- Screens no longer say You are now in. Focus moves straight to the first button.
- Quantity and price controls are now sliders. Swipe up or down to change them by one.
- Racks, floor boxes, and bills offer TalkBack actions, so you can stock, carry, or pay without opening them.
- Every card is read once, with no repeated announcements.
