# Grocery Simulator 3.4

A new Settings app for time format and game speed, a calmer game clock, a new Shop Floor status bar, and better TalkBack focus.

## Settings app

- The Computer has a new Settings app.
- Choose Time Format: 24-hour or 12-hour. Every time in the game switches at once, including shift and break times in HireNow.
- Choose Game Speed: Normal, Slow, or Fast. Normal is 10 game minutes for every real minute, Slow is 5, and Fast is 15.
- Each option is a radio button that TalkBack reads as selected or not selected. Your choices are saved with your game.

## Game time

- Time now passes more slowly: 10 game minutes per real minute at Normal speed.
- The clock only runs while you are on the Shop Floor or in the Warehouse. Using the Computer pauses it.

## Shop Floor status bar

- The Shop Floor status bar now shows cash on the left, the day in the middle, and the time on the right, in one row.
- TalkBack reads it as one item, for example: 5240 rupees. Day 3. 09:15.
- The clock no longer appears on HireNow pages. The Shop Floor is the only place that shows it.

## TalkBack focus

- When you open a page, focus now starts on the page title.
- When you go back, focus returns to the button or card you pressed on that page, for example Rice in MarketHub.
- The Back button in the toolbar is never focused automatically. Swipe to it when you need it.
